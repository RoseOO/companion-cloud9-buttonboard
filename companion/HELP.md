# Cloud9 Button Board

Companion surface integration for the Cloud9 / Symphony 24-button board
(model 0301, USB `2C82:012D`).

- 24 LCD keys appear as a 12×2 Companion grid.
- The 8 side keys are configured in the surface settings (page hotkeys,
  page up/down, home, or Companion button targets); they are not part of the
  page layout.
- Buttons show Companion's text and background colour on the 4-line LCD.
- Text alignment, Unicode punctuation normalisation and an optional
  page/row/column placeholder are available in the surface settings.
- The surface lock / PIN screen uses the left bank: 1-5 on top, 6-0 below,
  with the entry display and a `LOCKED` label.
- The board's corner page display follows the Companion page.
- Brightness controls the LCD backlight.

If the board is not detected, check that the surface integration is enabled
and press **Rescan USB** on the Surfaces page.
